Backtest 2022–2026 (projections made from prior-season data only): hitter wOBA error ties Steamer and beats ZiPS overall; trails Steamer by about 3% on hitters with 100–300 PA. Starters with 0–80 IP: K-BB% slightly better than Steamer, FIP slightly worse.
Playing-time ranges (P10–P90) captured 79–85% of actual outcomes in backtests. Fragility combines outcome spread and playing-time risk (hitters) or chance of falling short of 100 IP plus recent arm or shoulder IL time (pitchers).
Focus group: hitters with 100–300 PA and starters with 0–80 IP in 2026. Free agents (RosterResource tracker: certain or probable) get a neutral park and no org-specific context.
Full-time hitters and starters are projected at their recent healthy workload (minus a fitted gap for age, workload size and quality); injury risk shows in the full-season chance and the P10, not the headline. Left-handed hitters get a platoon role from a model of next year's starts vs lefty starters; individual platoon splits are regressed almost entirely to the league split. Minor leaguers and players who missed 2026 are included and labeled. Swingmen appear in both the starter and reliever lists.
